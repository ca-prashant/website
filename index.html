About Me

Dedicated Financial
Consultancy

I am Prashant Saud, a Chartered Accountant, auditor, and financial consultant based in Kathmandu, Nepal. With two years of rigorous hands-on experience as an Audit Associate during my articleship at ABS & ASSOCIATES, a highly respected audit firm in Nepal, I have built a solid foundation in statutory auditing, internal control assessments, and tax compliance.

My work has given me deep exposure to various industrial sectors, enabling me to handle complex compliance requirements, prepare accurate financial statements, and execute VAT and corporate tax filings with absolute technical rigor.

Whether you are transitioning to NFRS/IFRS, managing day-to-day corporate bookkeeping, or looking for strategic tax planning to optimize your liabilities, my objective is to provide tailored, penalty-free, and actionable financial solutions.

What I Offer

Core Services

Comprehensive financial and consulting services structured to comply with Nepalese tax laws and international accounting best practices.

01
Audit & Assurance

02
Tax Advisory & Compliance

Corporate and personal tax planning, timely income tax and VAT filings, and regulatory representation. Optimize tax structures while ensuring full compliance with Inland Revenue laws.

Accounting & Bookkeeping
